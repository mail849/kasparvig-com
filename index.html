Installation / Visual Work

Musikalske Balling
DR Koncerthuset

Co-producer and scenographer for Musikalske Balling at DR Koncerthuset, created in honour of film director Erik Balling’s 100th birthday. I designed and built a specially made set of ten projection frames for the concert. Main producer: Cæcilie Balling.

Christian Balvig & Ensemble Hermes
Musikhuset Aarhus

Scenography and live visuals for Christian Balvig and Ensemble Hermes at Musikhuset Aarhus. I created analog video animations from manipulated 8 mm film and archive footage, projected across a mosaic of screens around the ensemble.

Den fantastiske bus
The Royal Danish Theatre

Scenography and prop design for Den fantastiske bus at The Royal Danish Theatre. I designed and built the on-stage synthesizer for this theatre adaptation of Jakob Martin Strid’s book.

Girls in Airports
XENON, HUSET

Scenography for Girls in Airports’ residency at XENON during Copenhagen Jazz Festival, in collaboration with DIKTAKROM. A living lamp that pulsed with the music formed the centre of the installation.

EKKO PARK
Park Bio, Copenhagen

I initiated and curated EKKO PARK, a mini festival at Park Bio during Copenhagen Jazz Festival. The programme brought film, concerts and live visuals together, including Where Music Grows and concerts by Cæcilie Balling & Dybfølt, Troels Abrahamsen and Christian Balvig. I created the live visuals.
